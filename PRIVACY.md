# Gasha privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Gasha does not collect, sell, or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, and no server.

## What Gasha stores

Gasha saves the following with Chrome's extension storage on your own computer:

* The sites you choose to block
* Your notebook: the messages, video links, photos, and links you add to it
* Your settings and the times of your focus sessions, plus a count of finished sessions
* A salted hash of your password, if you set one. The password itself is never stored.

None of this leaves your computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server run by the developer.

## Your browsing

To block sites, Gasha compares the address of each page you open with your block list. This happens inside your browser. Addresses are not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

## Videos in your notebook

When your notebook shows a YouTube or TikTok video, your browser loads that video straight from YouTube or TikTok, the same way any embedded video loads on the web. Their own privacy policies apply to those videos.

## Backups

If you click **Download backup**, Gasha writes a file to your computer containing your blocked sites, notebook, and photos. Your password is never included. What happens to that file afterward is up to you.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling Gasha deletes everything it stored.
