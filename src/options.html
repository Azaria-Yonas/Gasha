Your shield against the sites you open on autopilot.

Welcome to Gasha

Gasha means shield in Amharic. It protects you from the sites you open out of muscle memory, like reaching for your phone in the middle of work. Three steps to set it up:

  1. Add the sites you open without thinking.
  2. Write your notebook. It is what you will see instead of those sites.
  3. Pin Gasha from the puzzle piece icon in your toolbar, then start a focus session.

Blocked sites

Add a domain like youtube.com to block the whole site, or a path like reddit.com/r/all to block only that part. Separate several with commas.

    Your notebook

    Blocked sites open your notebook instead. Fill it with notes to yourself, videos, photos, and links that pull you back to work. It saves on this computer and is there every time Chrome starts.

    When to block

    Password

    With a password, a focus session only ends early if someone types it. If you want to be extra strict, ask a friend or parent to set it for you.

    Protection

    Backup

    Your notebook, photos, and blocked sites are saved on this computer. Download a backup to keep a copy or move everything to another computer. Your password is not included.

    Gasha keeps everything on this computer. There is no account, and nothing is sent anywhere.
